Editorial case study cover, not a reproduction of the client's logo or website.

01 / THE BRIEF

Starting with the essentials.

The client arrived with a business name and initial reference materials, but needed a complete brand identity and professional online presence.

My role was to translate those starting points into a coherent visual direction, a fully developed website, and the foundational digital accounts needed to establish the brand online.

02 / THE CREATIVE APPROACH

Design choices with purpose.

I developed the logo and visual identity, choosing the brand's color direction with the healthcare industry and principles of color psychology in mind.

Working in a sensitive area of healthcare called for a considered balance: professional and credible, while remaining clear and approachable.

I also worked from the client's supplied reference materials to shape and organize the website's written content. The original subject-matter information came from the client; my contribution was adapting it for the website experience.

03 / THE WORK

One connected digital foundation.

01

Brand identity and logo

Developed the logo, color direction, and cohesive visual language for the brand.

02

Website design and development

Designed and built the complete website, translating the identity into an online experience.

03

Content adaptation

Turned the client's reference materials into organized, website-ready copy and presentation.

04

Domain and hosting

Handled domain registration, hosting configuration, and the technical setup required to launch.

05

Social media presence

Created the brand's social media accounts as part of establishing its digital footprint.

06

End-to-end delivery

Managed the project across design, content preparation, development, and launch rather than delivering an isolated website design.

04 / THE RESULT

A brand ready to show up online.

The result was a launched website and an integrated digital identity, giving MD Touch Sexual Health a consistent foundation across its web presence and newly established social accounts.

This project reflects the breadth of my approach: helping a client move from an initial idea to the visual, technical, and practical elements of a working digital presence.

Whether the brief calls for a straightforward business website or a more customized digital experience, the goal is the same: thoughtful design built around what the business actually needs.
