CASE STUDY / DIGITAL TRANSFORMATION

Beyond a website.
A connected digital ministry.

Church and Missions Aid Network · Live, ongoing development

01 / Understanding the need

CMAN serves an international community with recurring programs and spiritual resources. The opportunity was to make information easier to access while reducing repetitive administrative work.

02 / The strategic approach

Drawing on my administrative and operational experience, I approached the project as a digital platform rather than simply a visual redesign. The priorities were accessibility, visitor self-service and a cohesive experience tailored to the ministry.

03 / The tailored solution

A responsive website, interactive prayer time conversion, centralized prayer notes, leadership profiles and organized ministry resources. Further enhancements include a planned testimony experience and expanded resource discovery.

04 / The value

The platform is designed to simplify access to recurring resources, support international participation and reduce repetitive administrative tasks. These are intended operational benefits; quantified outcomes have not yet been measured.

CASE STUDY / E-COMMERCE + DIGITAL BRAND TRANSFORMATION

Beyond the storefront.
A connected brand experience.

Shaiglo Beautycare · Live website, ongoing marketing development

01 / Understanding the need

What began as a website improvement revealed a broader opportunity: create a cohesive skincare brand experience, simplify online shopping and connect customer education with marketing.

02 / The strategic approach

I approached the project from the perspective of the business and its customers, aligning the visual identity, shopping journey, founder-led education and promotional communications around Shaiglo’s specific needs.

03 / The tailored solution

A custom responsive WordPress storefront with refined product presentation, improved shopping layouts and tested promotional discount functionality. A coordinated Black Friday and Cyber Monday campaign connected website and social creative, while founder-led educational content was developed into three new published journal articles. Supporting work included search-oriented content, branded video editing and email marketing foundations.

04 / The value

The project delivers a more cohesive brand presence, clearer shopping experience and a growing educational resource for customers. It establishes a foundation for future marketing and discoverability without claiming unmeasured sales or traffic gains.

The website and journal are live. Email campaigns and welcome automation are prepared but not yet confirmed active; full payment transaction testing remains outstanding.

From visibility to engagement.
Connecting a growing professional community.

01 / Understanding the need

A national network of over 2,000 members serving Nigerian healthcare professionals and their families in Canada needed a stronger digital presence and more intentional engagement with younger doctors and international medical graduates.

02 / The strategic approach

Developed audience-focused communications across the organization and its youth and young leaders arm, strengthening social channels, event visibility and the presentation of sponsorship opportunities.

03 / The tailored solution

Optimized Facebook and Instagram, created promotional campaigns, CME flyers, sponsorship materials and conference content. Provided on-site coverage in Winnipeg in 2024 and Edmonton in 2025; delivered pre-event marketing for Halifax in 2026. Designed the inaugural 148-page lifestyle magazine in 2026.

04 / The value

Grew the social audience from approximately 200 to over 1,000 followers, supported engagement with younger professionals and international medical graduates, and strengthened sponsorship communications. Audience and membership figures are based on reported project results.
