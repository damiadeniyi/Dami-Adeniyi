FEATURED CASE STUDY / WEB EXPERIENCE + EVENT TECHNOLOGY

Becoming the Dadas.

A wedding invitation that opens into a world of its own. A beautifully considered celebration experience, powered by custom guest-management technology.

CREATIVE DIRECTION & UI/UXWEB DEVELOPMENTRSVP SYSTEMS & CLOUD INTEGRATION
01 / THE BRIEF

More than a wedding website.

Oluseyi and Oluwaseun's celebration spans a traditional wedding, a wedding ceremony and reception, and a Thanksgiving gathering in Victoria, British Columbia. Guests are traveling from across Canada and around the world.

The challenge was to create a unified, elegant digital home for multiple celebrations while making invitations, travel information, guest communication, and attendance management intuitive.

02 / THE EXPERIENCE

Spring Opulence, made interactive.

01

An invitation you open

The experience begins with an invitation-style entry that opens into the homepage, turning a simple website visit into a moment of anticipation.

02

A celebration with its own identity

Spring florals, warm ivory, deep plum, blush, lilac, muted gold, refined typography, an intertwined OO monogram, and subtle movement give the experience its personality.

03

Three celebrations, one home

Traditional wedding, ceremony and reception, and Thanksgiving information live together, complemented by dress inspiration, travel and accommodation guidance, FAQs, and the couple's story.

04

Personal touches

Gift information, a thank-you register, and moderated letters to the couple make space for meaningful guest interaction beyond attendance.

03 / THE ENGINE

Behind the romance, a purpose-built RSVP system.

Instead of a generic form, I designed a household-based invitation flow that recognizes exactly who has been invited and lets each person respond for eligible events.

01Invitation email

A branded invitation invites guests into the website.

02Verified access

The primary contact enters their registered email and receives a six-digit verification code.

03Household recognition

After verification, the invitation displays the named guests associated with that household.

04Individual responses

Each invited guest can respond to relevant celebrations, rather than applying one answer to everyone.

05Private administration

Responses are stored for review through a protected administration dashboard.

04 / THE SYSTEM

Designed front to back.

Cloudflare Pages + functions

Public website, hosting, and server-side workflows connected to the guest experience.

Cloudflare D1

Live records for households, invited individuals, verification, RSVPs, and guest messages, separate from public website files.

Google Sheets

Structured guest-list planning and approval, with household and individual guest records. Changes require reconciliation with the live database.

Gmail API

Personalized HTML invitations and email verification through a dedicated wedding sender, with controlled test sending.

Protected admin area

RSVP monitoring, moderation of Letters to the Dadas, and a Gift Thank-You Register behind Cloudflare Access.

Thoughtful privacy

Private guest information is handled through backend systems rather than exposed in the public site.

05 / PROGRESS & LEARNINGS

A working foundation, with a careful launch process.

The website, household RSVP flow, and admin dashboard have been developed and tested. Personalized invitation emails have also been successfully tested with the couple.

Before the full guest invitation launch, the final guest list must be reconciled, live database records synchronized, invitation personalization completed, and deliverability verified. The production invitation campaign has not yet launched.

The bigger lesson: the most memorable digital experiences are not just beautiful. They connect storytelling, information architecture, and reliable systems in service of real people.
