FEATURED CASE STUDY / BESPOKE WEDDING EXPERIENCE

Becoming the Dadas.

A wedding invitation that opens into a world of its own. A beautifully considered celebration experience, with thoughtful details that make planning easier for the couple and their guests.

CREATIVE DIRECTION & UI/UXWEB DEVELOPMENTPERSONALIZED GUEST EXPERIENCE
01 / THE BRIEF

More than a wedding website.

Oluseyi and Oluwaseun's celebration spans a traditional wedding, a wedding ceremony and reception, and a Thanksgiving gathering in Victoria, British Columbia. Guests are traveling from across Canada and around the world.

The challenge was to create a unified, elegant digital home for multiple celebrations while making invitations, travel information, guest communication, and attendance management intuitive.

02 / THE EXPERIENCE

Spring Opulence, made interactive.

01

An invitation you open

The experience begins with an invitation-style entry that opens into the homepage, turning a simple website visit into a moment of anticipation.

02

A celebration with its own identity

Spring florals, warm ivory, deep plum, blush, lilac, muted gold, refined typography, an intertwined OO monogram, and subtle movement give the experience its personality.

03

Three celebrations, one home

Traditional wedding, ceremony and reception, and Thanksgiving information live together, complemented by dress inspiration, travel and accommodation guidance, FAQs, and the couple's story.

04

Personal touches

Gift information, a thank-you register, and moderated letters to the couple make space for meaningful guest interaction beyond attendance.

03 / THE SOLUTION

Personal by design. Practical by intention.

A memorable wedding website should do more than look beautiful. It should help guests feel welcomed, informed, and confident about the celebrations ahead.

Personalized invitations

A branded digital invitation welcomes guests into the couple's story and guides them toward the information they need.

Thoughtful household RSVPs

Invited households can respond for their named guests, making attendance planning more personal and organized than a generic RSVP form.

Multiple events, one experience

Guests can explore the distinct celebrations and respond to relevant events without juggling separate websites or invitations.

Meaningful guest interaction

Travel guidance, frequently asked questions, gift information, and moderated well wishes make the site a useful destination throughout the celebration journey.

04 / THE APPROACH

Creativity meets problem-solving.

I led the visual design and website development, translating the couple's floral inspiration into a cohesive digital identity that feels intimate, elegant, and distinctly theirs.

I also developed custom RSVP and guest-management functionality to support the practical needs of a multi-event wedding, keeping the guest experience simple while giving the couple a more organized way to manage responses.

The result is a personalized wedding experience that balances storytelling, usability, and behind-the-scenes functionality.

05 / THE RESULT

A celebration with a digital home of its own.

The live website brings the couple's story, celebration details, guest information, and personalized RSVP experience together in one place.

The invitation and RSVP experience have been tested, while preparations for the full guest invitation rollout continue ahead of the wedding.

This project reflects how I approach custom websites: every design decision should feel personal, and every feature should solve a real need.
